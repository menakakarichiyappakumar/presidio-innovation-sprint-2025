Finance Documentation:

How to file a reimbursement:
1. Log in to the expense management system.
2. Create a new expense report.
3. Attach all original receipts.
4. Submit for approval to your manager.
5. Reimbursements are processed within 5-7 business days.

Last month's budget report:
- Can be found on the Finance SharePoint site under 'Monthly Reports'.
- Access requires finance department permissions.

When is payroll processed:
- Payroll is processed on the 15th and 30th of each month.
- If these dates fall on a weekend or holiday, payroll is processed on the preceding business day.
