**HR Policy Document**
**Policy Name:** Employee Leave Policy
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All Full-Time Employees

### 1. Purpose

To outline the leave entitlements and procedures for all eligible employees.

### 2. Types of Leave

* **Casual Leave (CL):** 12 days/year
* **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 days/year
* **Earned Leave (EL):** 15 days/year (accrued monthly)
* **Maternity Leave:** 26 weeks (as per Maternity Benefit Act)
* **Paternity Leave:** 5 days

### 3. Leave Application

* Must be submitted via HRMS at least 3 days in advance (except for emergencies).
* Medical certificate is required for SL beyond 2 consecutive days.

### 4. Leave Encashment

* Only EL is encashable during separation or as per company guidelines.

### 5. Unplanned Absence

* Uninformed absence for 3 consecutive days will be treated as abandonment of service.
